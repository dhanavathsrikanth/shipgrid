# Pricing — ShipGrid

ShipGrid is **free** for builders and buyers. The platform is community-driven with optional premium features planned.

## Free (Default)

- Price: $0/month
- Submit unlimited products
- Enable waitlist pages with ICP-declared signups
- 72-hour beta launch window with ICP-matched feed promotion
- Build logs / changelog feed
- Follower notifications
- Founder analytics dashboard
- AI-generated FAQ on product pages
- Personalized "For You" feed
- Email digest with matched recommendations
- Comment, vote, rate, bookmark
